4-Pin RGB Bulb Leg Identification

Hold the bulb with legs facing down and the flat edge of the rim on the left:

Pin 1 RED (+) 220Ω Resistor
Pin 2 (Longest) ANODE (+) Direct to +3.3V Rail
Pin 3 GREEN (+) 220Ω Resistor
Pin 4 BLUE (+) 220Ω Resistor

Ultrasonic Sensor Echo 3.3V Safety

HC-SR04 operates at 5V. In physical breadboard builds, protect GPIO 18 by dropping Echo to 3.3V:

Echo Pin ──[ 1kΩ Resistor ]──┬──> ESP32 GPIO 18
│
[ 2kΩ Resistor ]
│
GND

In Wokwi simulation, you can connect Echo straight to GPIO 18 without damage.
